# Drill: Policy or Mechanism?

*Chapter 2 · Part 6: Design and Implementation.*

A classic exam question, drilled. For each item, decide: is it a policy (what will be done) or a mechanism (how to do it)?

This quiz mixes multiple choice, true or false, and typed answers (9 questions). Each answer is checked as soon as you give it. Typed answers ignore capitals and extra spaces. A correct answer comes with a short explanation, and a wrong one costs nothing, so try again.
