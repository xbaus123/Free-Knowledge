# Trends: Cognitive Radio and AI

*Chapter 11 · Wireless and IoT.*

The radio spectrum is crowded, and wireless networks keep getting busier. Two trends are using software, and increasingly AI, to squeeze more out of the air.

## Software-defined radio

A traditional radio does its work in fixed hardware built for one band and one protocol. A **software-defined radio (SDR)** does most of that work, like tuning and modulation, in **software**. The same hardware can change frequency, or even protocol, with a software update.

## Cognitive radio

A **cognitive radio** is a smart SDR. It uses AI to:

1. **Sense** the spectrum around it.
2. **Find unused portions**: frequencies nobody is using right now, sometimes called **white space**.
3. **Move there automatically**, to improve performance and avoid interference.

It relies on SDR, because only a software radio can change its frequency on the fly.

## AI-based optimization

Enterprise Wi-Fi systems now use AI to tune the network as it runs:

1. **Collect** traffic data from every access point.
2. Build an **operational profile**: what normal looks like, by time of day.
3. **Look for anomalies**: signs of congestion or interference.
4. **Actively adjust** to what's happening: change channels, transmit power, or which band clients use.

It's chapter 10's baselines and IoT's known-good profile again, closing the loop: when the network notices it's not normal, it fixes itself.
